# 📊 Retail Like-for-Like (LFL) Sales Analysis | Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard comparing retail performance of **2026 vs 2025**
(Jan–May 2026) across regions, cities, store formats, departments and categories.

## 🎯 Objective
Understand which regions, store formats, departments and categories drive business
growth, and how each is performing against the previous year on a like-for-like basis.

## 📈 Key Metrics (Grocery & Staples view)
| Metric | Current Year | Previous Year | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | 92.7M | 77.4M | ▲ 19.7% |
| Transactions | 215,887 | 173,016 | ▲ 24.8% |
| Avg Transaction Value | 429 | 447 | ▼ 4.1% |
| Quantity | 640K | 474K | ▲ 35.1% |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **North** is the top region at 23.8M sales (+19.6%); Central is the smallest at 14.5M.
- **Supermarket** format contributes 62.44% of sales, followed by Express (22.22%) and Fresh (15.34%).
- **Rice & Grains** is the largest category (32.9M); **Sugar & Salt** shows the fastest growth (+44%).
- Growth is **volume-led**: transactions and quantity are up, but ATV and average selling price are down.
